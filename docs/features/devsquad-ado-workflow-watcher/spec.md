## Executive Summary

**Approved specification amendment (2026-09-10):** D1-B (authorized first-seen admission), D2 matching with D2-A (eligibility pause/reentry), and D3-A (bounded restart-from-beginning discovery) are approved requirements for existing slice 14 / #21, **unimplemented and not independently reviewed**. The W038 technical PASS above covers the historical supplied-candidate implementation and recovery contract only; it does not cover this extension. This is the specification checkpoint, not a plan, ADR acceptance, implementation authorization, merge-readiness claim, or permission to start slice 15.

- **Objective:** Give a DevSquad host a deterministic, bounded watcher that observes injected ADO/GitHub work-item and pull-request signals, applies host-defined discovery and eligibility policy, and records durable coordination outcomes without owning the DevSquad lifecycle.
- **Primary user:** A DevSquad host coordinator that supplies candidates or an explicitly bounded host-stable discovery scope, matching policy, admission authorization, exact initial phase/status and intake rules, and consumes typed intake signals.
- **Value delivered:** The reviewed supplied-candidate pass retains fenced checkpoints, at-most-once batched intake, bounded scheduling, and honest cooperative cancellation. The approved extension adds offline, host-invoked discovery/filtering and authorized first-seen admission, not autonomous orchestration.
- **Scope:** Includes injected read-oriented, transport-neutral discovery/observation seams, deterministic matching before privacy projection, authorized initialization through the existing ledger, fenced existing-record mutations, durable per-item cursors, bounded traversal/polling and typed operations; excludes live network access, lifecycle authority, agent execution, and pull-request authority.
- **Primary success criterion:** Fixed injected inputs, clock, policy version and durable state yield deterministic decisions. Comment intake follows a validated durable checkpoint; separately typed discovery-only intake follows validated first-initialization acceptance and is reported at most once across concurrency, retry and restart. Both are at-most-once, not lossless; durable-but-unacknowledged writes or host crashes can lose signals and require host reconciliation.

## Objective

Add a host-side workflow watcher that turns injected external observations into deterministic, durably checkpointed intake signals for DevSquad.

The watcher runs one bounded, explicitly host-invoked pass in one of two modes. **Supplied-candidate observation** retains the reviewed behavior: read existing records for a caller-supplied set, evaluate new activity, claim and checkpoint cursors, then return comment intake. **Discovery** traverses an explicitly bounded host-stable scope/partition from its beginning on every invocation, applies caller matching policy, admits eligible first-seen items only with explicit host authorization and initial phase/status, and observes eligible existing items through their durable anchors. Discovery-only admission is not a comment event and requires no fabricated cursor or claim.

The watcher is an observation and coordination component. It is not a scheduler of agents, not an owner of DevSquad phase semantics, and not a client of any live tracker service.

## Context

Slice 13 delivered the persistent DevSquad/ADO workflow ledger (`openDevSquadAdoWorkflowLedger`) and ADR-0025, which established the ledger as an offline, fenced, repository-local coordination checkpoint with claims, leases, fencing values, idempotency receipts, and strict fail-closed recovery.

The ledger deliberately does not observe anything. It stores opaque work-item comment cursors and pull-request thread/comment cursors, but nothing in Sandcastle currently decides _when_ a record should be revisited, _which_ external events are new, or _how_ to bound that work.

Existing ADO components already establish the injected control-plane pattern: `AdoControlPlaneFactory` distinguishes `read-only` from `write` access and validates injected client methods, and `runAdoFeedbackLoop` accepts a structurally validated control-plane-like object with bounded observation counts. Those components observe a single already-published pull request; they do not maintain durable per-work-item cursors, claims, or restart-safe intake decisions.

Without this slice, a host coordinator must hand-roll polling, deduplication, claim handling, and termination logic around the ledger, which is exactly where double-processing, unbounded loops, and stale-owner writes appear.

## Scope

### Included

- One bounded watch pass over a caller-supplied candidate set.
- An alternative discovery pass over injected pages in a host-declared stable scope/partition; no durable page continuation.
- Host-defined exact matching for state, tags, area, iteration and host-resolved team membership, with policy-versioned minimized evidence.
- Authorized first-seen initialization through `initializeRecord`, including items with no comments, and separately typed at-most-once discovery-only intake.
- Eligibility exit pauses observation with cursors preserved; reentry resumes the durable anchor or reports explicit retention loss.
- An injected, read-oriented ADO/GitHub observation seam with no live transport.
- Deterministic eligibility evaluation with stable reason codes and stable ordering.
- Ledger claim acquisition, renewal (heartbeat), and release around watcher actions.
- Fencing-protected, revision-checked, idempotent ledger checkpoints written by the watcher.
- Durable persistence of last-seen work-item comment and pull-request thread/comment cursors.
- New-event selection relative to persisted cursors, without retaining external bodies.
- Comment and discovery-only intake signals emitted only when caller-supplied phase/status intake rules match; discovery admission additionally requires explicit host authorization.
- Deterministic bounded polling, deterministic backoff, and cooperative cancellation.
- Typed operations, structured errors, and a token-free public result projection.
- Deterministic tests using injected observations, injected clock, injected timers, and isolated ledger fixtures.

### Excluded

- Live ADO, GitHub, MCP, REST, Azure CLI, Azure DevOps CLI, or other network calls.
- Owning, defining, or validating the DevSquad phase state machine or transition legality.
- Deciding whether a work item should be worked on as a product/priority matter.
- Selecting, assigning, or claiming work items in the external tracker.
- Creating, updating, approving, completing, or merging pull requests.
- Posting external comments or work-item updates.
- Running agents, creating sandboxes, creating worktrees, or executing git operations.
- Storing comment bodies, review bodies, prompts, credentials, tokens, or agent-session contents.
- Long-lived daemon/service hosting, process supervision, or cross-machine scheduling.
- Modifying ledger schema v1, its durability protocol, or its capacity ceilings.
- Persisting discovery page continuations, adding a sidecar/outbox, or repurposing comment cursors for traversal, eligibility or admission markers.
- Modernizing existing ADO control-plane, feedback-loop, team-runner, or execution-adapter APIs.

## Actors

- **DevSquad host coordinator:** Invokes one mode, supplies candidates or a bounded stable discovery scope/partition, versioned matching policy, admission authorization and initial phase/status, intake rules, budgets and owner identity, and consumes typed intake signals. Selection policy and implementation authorization remain the host's decisions.
- **DevSquad lifecycle owner:** Decides what an intake signal means, whether a phase may change, and what runs next.
- **Injected discovery/observation seams:** Supply stable pages, host-normalized matching facts and host-resolved team membership, plus anchored work-item and pull-request observations, through transport-neutral host implementations or test fakes. Sandcastle constructs no live client.
- **Workflow ledger:** Owns durability, claims, fencing, idempotency, and recovery semantics from slice 13.
- **Operator:** Diagnoses why a work item was skipped, deferred, conflicted, or reported as recovery-blocked.

## User Scenarios and Tests

### Scenario 1: New work-item comment becomes one intake signal

**Given** a ledger record for work item `137` whose last-seen work-item comment cursor is `480`, and injected observations containing comments `480` and `481`,
**When** a watch pass runs and the record's phase/status match the caller's intake rules,
**Then** exactly one intake signal is emitted describing new work-item activity, and the record's cursor is durably advanced to `481` before the pass reports success.

### Scenario 2: Already-observed events do not repeat

**Given** the ledger record from Scenario 1 after the cursor advanced to `481`,
**When** a second watch pass runs against the identical injected observations,
**Then** no intake signal is emitted, no checkpoint is written, and the record revision is unchanged.

### Scenario 3: Concurrent watchers cannot both act

**Given** two watch passes with different owner identities target the same work item while no claim has expired,
**When** their claim acquisitions overlap,
**Then** exactly one pass acts and the other reports a `claim-conflict` skip for that candidate without mutating the record and without aborting its remaining candidates.

### Scenario 4: Stale watcher cannot write after takeover

**Given** a watcher's claim lease expires and another owner takes over with a higher fencing value,
**When** the former watcher attempts to checkpoint an observed cursor,
**Then** the ledger rejects the mutation as stale, the watcher surfaces a stale-ownership outcome, and no cursor is advanced.

### Scenario 5: Bounded scheduling and cooperative cancellation

**Given** a watch pass configured with a maximum poll count, a deterministic backoff schedule, and a cancellation signal,
**When** no candidate becomes eligible, or the signal is aborted mid-pass,
**Then** no further poll is scheduled beyond the budget, each observation receives cancellation, and every candidate reports cleanup evidence. Termination assumes ledger operations and delays settle; noncooperating dependencies cannot be forcibly terminated.

### Scenario 6: Phase rules gate intake, not lifecycle meaning

**Given** a record whose phase is `awaiting-approval` and caller intake rules that only admit `implement` and `review`,
**When** new external activity is observed,
**Then** the cursor is still advanced durably, the candidate is reported as intake-suppressed with a stable reason, and no intake signal is emitted; Sandcastle applies no phase ordering of its own.

### Scenario 7: Authorized discovery without comments

**Given** first-seen item `999` matches the configured policy, has no comments, and has explicit host admission authorization and initial phase/status admitted by intake rules,
**When** discovery passes overlap, retry, or restart,
**Then** initialization uses only existing `initializeRecord`; at most one validated fresh acceptance returns a typed discovery-only intake. Replay, existing-record results and ambiguous acknowledgements never authorize another discovery signal. Without authorization, initialization and discovery intake counts are zero; no default lifecycle state, cursor or claim is invented.

### Scenario 8: Eligibility exit preserves the reentry anchor

**Given** item `137` has cursor `480`, becomes excluded by matching policy, and comment `481` arrives,
**When** discovery excludes the item and later it reenters eligibility,
**Then** exclusion performs no comment/PR observation or mutation and leaves cursor `480` unchanged. Reentry observes from `480` and advances to `481` only after fenced acceptance, or reports explicit `observation-anchor-missing` for retention loss. This differs from Scenario 6 / FR-042, which advances cursors for an otherwise eligible item whose phase/status suppress intake.

### Scenario 9: Bounded multi-page discovery and restart

**Given** a stable host scope contains items `137` and `138` across multiple pages, including an empty page with a continuation,
**When** traversal fits the declared bounds,
**Then** every discovered item receives a disposition and traversal reports complete only at an explicit terminal page. The next invocation starts at the beginning and uses durable per-item cursors without duplicating acknowledged comment intake. With page budget `2`, at most two page calls are initiated and any remaining continuation yields explicit incomplete status, not silent success or saved continuation.

## Functional Requirements

### Watch Pass and Typed Operations

- **FR-001:** The feature shall expose a typed operation that runs exactly one bounded watch pass and resolves with a structured result or a structured error, without throwing untyped errors for expected conditions.
- **FR-002:** A watch pass shall accept an opened workflow ledger, injected read seams, an owner identifier, a host-supplied stable pass identity, intake rules, budgets, an injected UTC clock/delay and optional cancellation. The request selects exactly one mode: the existing supplied candidate set, or discovery with an explicitly bounded host-stable scope/partition, matching-policy version and optional explicit admission authorization with initial phase/status. No discovery configuration changes supplied-candidate semantics.
- **FR-003:** All watch inputs shall be validated before any injected side effects, including clock, delay, seam, or ledger invocation. An optional signal must expose boolean `aborted` and callable `addEventListener` and `removeEventListener`.
- **FR-004:** Validation shall reject a blank owner identifier, an empty or duplicate canonical supplied candidate set, invalid work-item identifiers, invalid budgets, lease durations, backoff parameters, and intake rules. Supplied candidates number 1–1,000 inclusive; `maxPolls` is a safe integer 1–10,000 inclusive; durations are positive safe integers, lease is at most 86,400,000ms, and the positive renewal threshold is strictly below the lease. No duplicate candidate is silently deduplicated. Discovery may return zero candidates; its mode, scope, policy/operators and declared page/item/collection/UTF-8 bounds are validated before injected effects (FR-061–064).
- **FR-005:** The watch result shall report, per candidate, a stable outcome discriminator, a stable reason code, and the resulting durable revision when a mutation was accepted.
- **FR-006:** The watch result shall report pass-level counts, polls, and a stable stop reason. `acted` counts returned intake signals of either type; `suppressed` counts acknowledged suppressed cursor advances, not eligibility pauses; `failed` counts final failed candidates and may overlap acted/suppressed. `cleanupReleased`, `cleanupFailed`, `cleanupIndeterminate`, and `cleanupNotRequired` partition all candidate outcomes. Discovery additionally reports page calls, discovered/evaluated/admitted/paused item counts and explicit traversal completeness/reason; initialization without intake is not counted as a suppressed cursor advance.
- **FR-007:** A failure affecting one candidate shall not abort the remaining candidates unless the failure is a pass-level fault (invalid input, cancellation, ledger-open failure, budget exhaustion, or discovery traversal invalidity). Missing matching facts are a per-candidate fail-closed disposition, not a reason to abort otherwise valid items.
- **FR-008:** Public watcher operations and types shall be exported through the package's established public entry point.

### Injected Observation Seam

- **FR-009:** The watcher shall obtain every external observation through an injected seam and shall never construct a transport, client, credential, or URL of its own.
- **FR-010:** The seam shall be read-oriented: it shall expose only observation methods and shall never be invoked to update work items, post comments, or mutate pull requests.
- **FR-011:** The watcher shall structurally validate the seam before use and shall return a stable configuration error naming the missing method when a required method is absent.
- **FR-012:** Observation cursor and discovery continuation identifiers shall remain opaque: the watcher shall not parse, sort or arithmetically compare them. Work-item identity alone uses the existing canonicalization and deterministic ordering contract in FR-018, including IDs obtained from discovery; matching values use only FR-065's explicit equality/path-segment predicates, not inferred identifier semantics.
- **FR-013:** Seam rejections shall be converted into stable per-candidate observation errors, or explicit incomplete traversal errors for discovery page failures, without raw transport messages, URLs with credentials, matching facts or external bodies.
- **FR-014:** The watcher shall call comment/PR observation seams at most once per eligible candidate per poll for each observation kind it needs. Discovery page calls are separately bounded by FR-063; eligibility-paused items require no comment/PR calls.
- **FR-015:** The watcher shall not require pull-request observations for a candidate that has no persisted pull-request identifier.

### Deterministic Eligibility

- **FR-016:** Eligibility shall be a deterministic function of the candidate identifier, the durable ledger record, the injected observations, the caller configuration, and the single clock reading for that poll.
- **FR-017:** The watcher shall read the injected clock once per poll and shall use that value for every eligibility, lease, and timestamp decision in the poll.
- **FR-018:** Supplied candidates shall retain stable canonical work-item ordering independent of input ordering. Discovery shall follow the host-stable page traversal, evaluating each validated page's items in canonical work-item order without parsing opaque IDs. Identical scope, pages and policy yield identical call/decision order; no global input-order-independence is claimed across different page partitions. Repeated canonical items within one traversal are invalid, not silently deduplicated.
- **FR-019:** Every eligibility decision shall carry a stable reason code, including for ineligibility.
- **FR-020:** In supplied-candidate mode, a missing ledger record remains `skipped` / `record-not-found`, with no initialization. In discovery mode only, an eligible first-seen item may be initialized through existing `initializeRecord` with explicit host admission authorization and explicit initial phase/status (FR-067–069); absent authorization means zero initialization and no discovery-only intake.
- **FR-021:** A candidate shall be ineligible when an unexpired claim is held by another owner, reported as a claim conflict rather than a fault.
- **FR-022:** Comment-observation action in either mode requires an external event newer than its persisted cursor. Discovery-only first admission is a distinct action whose eligibility is policy match plus host authorization, not a new-comment predicate; it may occur with no comments and creates no comment checkpoint.
- **FR-023:** Eligibility shall not be inferred from phase or status names beyond the caller-supplied intake rules.
- **FR-024:** Repeating a watch pass with identical inputs and unchanged durable state shall produce an identical decision set.

### Claims, Leases, and Fencing

- **FR-025:** Before checkpointing an existing record, the watcher shall acquire a ledger claim using the owner identifier, a freshly generated capability token, and the caller-supplied lease duration; renewal/release retain their existing authority requirements. The sole creation exception is discovery admission via claim-free `initializeRecord`, as already defined by ADR-0025. Initialization grants no claim authority and cannot overwrite an existing record.
- **FR-026:** The watcher shall generate claim tokens from a cryptographically random source and shall never log, return, persist, or embed a token in results, errors, or diagnostics.
- **FR-027:** The watcher shall carry the acquired fencing value on every subsequent mutation for that candidate within the pass.
- **FR-028:** When a pass spans more than one poll while holding a claim, the watcher shall renew the claim before the lease reaches its inclusive expiry.
- **FR-029:** At candidate completion, cancellation, or error the watcher shall invoke release exactly once for previously validated authority, retaining local authority evidence until the response is validated. It shall never retry release, guess authority, reacquire for cleanup, or report release without a validated acknowledgement. Without validated authority there shall be zero release calls. Every candidate shall carry the mandatory cleanup object defined below; inclusive lease expiry remains the backstop, not proof of release.
- **FR-030:** A `claim-conflict`, `claim-expired`, `stale-fencing`, or `claim-authorization` result shall be surfaced as a distinct stable outcome and shall never be retried by silently re-acquiring within the same candidate step.
- **FR-031:** The watcher shall not delete, reset, or force-release a claim owned by another owner.

### Cursor and Observation Persistence

- **FR-032:** Newly observed work-item comment cursors and pull-request thread/comment cursors shall be persisted through ledger checkpoints that supply the expected revision, expected phase, and expected status.
- **FR-033:** The watcher shall persist only opaque identifiers and shall never persist comment bodies, review bodies, authors, prompts, credentials, or tokens.
- **FR-034:** A pull-request cursor shall be persisted only when both thread and comment identifiers are available; a kind reported in `skippedCursorKinds` shall never also appear in `cursorChanges` for the same outcome.
- **FR-035:** The watcher shall select the next cursor from seam ordering only, never sort or numerically order observation identifiers. Work-item identity is exact `commentId`; PR identity is exact `(threadId, commentId)` after only missing/undefined/null comment normalization to null. Reject every duplicate identity. Blank comments are invalid identifiers, not missing comments. Mixed new PR entries advance to the newest complete pair; mark PR skipped only when none of the new entries is persistable.
- **FR-035b:** Each work-item or PR window has inclusive limits of 1,000 entries and 1,048,576 aggregate UTF-8 identifier bytes; PR bytes sum `threadId` and nonnull `commentId`. Each identifier remains limited to 1,024 UTF-8 bytes. Check count before iteration/copy and accumulate bytes and uniqueness before retaining each entry, with no oversized full projection or truncation. Malformed windows, duplicates, or aggregate/count excess fail the entire candidate observation as `failed` / `invalid-observation-window`, even when the other kind is valid. Individual identifier violations remain `invalid-observation-identifier`. The host SHOULD bound upstream responses; the watcher MUST validate independently. The WI aggregate limit is redundant with 1,000 × 1,024 = 1,024,000 bytes; use PR windows for independently reachable aggregate-boundary tests, without relaxing either WI bound.
- **FR-035a:** When a persisted cursor anchor is absent from a non-empty seam window, the watcher shall fail that candidate closed with a stable anchor-loss outcome and shall advance no cursor.
- **FR-036:** Cursor advancement shall be acknowledged as durable before the corresponding intake signal is reported as delivered by the pass result.
- **FR-036a:** Before checkpointing, the watcher shall verify that the observation anchors and pull-request identity read before the claim are unchanged in the record returned by the acquisition; if they moved, it shall release the claim, advance no cursor, and report a stable stale-observation outcome.
- **FR-037:** Every watcher mutation shall supply an operation identifier derived from canonically serialized identity: a checkpoint identifier scoped to the exact cursor advance it publishes, so retrying an ambiguous mutation replays the original outcome; a claim-lifecycle identifier scoped to a random per-acquisition claim epoch, so a retry under the same pass identity can always acquire again; and, in discovery mode, an initialization identifier bound to the original authorized admission request, stable across its retries and never reused for changed input. Discovery retries preserve the original submission; they do not invent a new identity to bypass conflict or duplicate reporting.
- **FR-037a:** The claim epoch shall be an idempotency namespace only; it shall never be a capability, and capability tokens shall remain independently random.
- **FR-038:** An `idempotency-conflict` result shall be surfaced as a distinct stable outcome and shall not be resolved by generating a different operation identifier within the same step.
- **FR-039:** A `revision-conflict` or `state-conflict` result shall end that candidate's step with a stable conflict outcome, leaving durable state unchanged.

### DevSquad Intake Boundary

- **FR-040:** The watcher shall emit intake signals only; it shall never invoke DevSquad, transition a phase, or write phase/status values not supplied by the caller.
- **FR-041:** An intake signal shall be emitted only when the record's current phase and status satisfy the caller-supplied intake rules.
- **FR-042:** For otherwise observation-eligible records, unmatched phase/status intake rules still yield intake suppression while persisting new cursors. Discovery matching exclusion instead pauses observation and preserves cursors (FR-066); these reasons and effects are distinct. A newly initialized record whose phase/status are not admitted returns no discovery-only intake and fabricates no cursor advance.
- **FR-043:** Intake rules shall be exact-match sets supplied by the caller; Sandcastle shall define no default phase list, ordering, or terminal state.
- **FR-044:** Comment intake shall retain work-item identity, pre-acquire source revision, changed observation kinds, exact phase/status and token-free claim metadata. The separately discriminated discovery-only intake shall instead identify the work item, accepted initialization revision, exact host-supplied phase/status and minimized policy-versioned admission evidence. It has no changed-comment kinds, comment checkpoint, pre-acquire source revision or claim metadata; none may be fabricated.
- **FR-045:** Intake signals shall contain no external bodies, no claim tokens, and no agent-session contents.
- **FR-046:** The watcher shall emit at most one intake signal per candidate per pass across both types. A first-seen initialization ends that candidate's step, leaving genuine comment observation to a later invocation; no comment cursor is seeded to suppress unseen comments.

### Bounded Polling, Backoff, and Cancellation

- **FR-047:** A supplied-candidate pass retains its first-of termination: all candidates resolved, maximum polls, poll-start elapsed budget, or cancellation. Discovery may claim traversal complete only after an explicit terminal page and dispositions for all its items; resolved items on a nonterminal page do not end traversal. Exhausted page/item/poll/time bounds, invalid traversal or cancellation return explicit incomplete status with retained per-item outcomes, not a durable continuation.
- **FR-048:** Maximum polls and the poll-start elapsed budget shall be validated and enforced before each additional poll. The elapsed budget bounds poll scheduling, not runtime or physical requests. Ledger operations are awaited rather than abandoned; liveness requires them and injected delays to settle.
- **FR-049:** Delays between polls shall be produced by an injected delay source so tests are deterministic and require no wall-clock waiting.
- **FR-050:** Backoff shall follow a deterministic schedule derived from a base interval, multiplier, and maximum interval, with no randomness unless an explicit jitter source is injected.
- **FR-051:** Cancellation shall be freshly checked immediately before each seam call, before each non-cleanup ledger mutation, and between polls. A work-item seam that returns valid data while aborting the parent must prevent PR invocation. Mandatory cleanup is not skipped because the parent is aborted.
- **FR-052:** Cancellation shall still attempt cleanup for validated authority and report acknowledged checkpoint or initialization outcomes and their permitted signals; release cannot retract or roll back them.
- **FR-053:** Each observation shall have a dedicated AbortController passed into the seam and linked to its parent. Timeout or abort cancels the seam and timer; every terminal path removes links/listeners and consumes or quarantines late settlements so they cannot change outcomes or create unhandled rejections. Timeout presumes a valid settling delay. Cancellation is cooperative, not forcible termination or an unconditional runtime/physical-request bound.

### Recovery and Fail-Closed Behavior

- **FR-054:** Ledger recovery errors (`corrupt-artifact`, `unsupported-schema-version`, `path-boundary`, `unsupported-permissions`, `capacity-exceeded`, `scan-limit`) shall be surfaced with their stable ledger category and shall not be repaired, reset, or bypassed by the watcher.
- **FR-055:** A record blocked by a recovery error shall be reported as a failed candidate and shall not prevent other candidates from being processed.
- **FR-056:** The watcher shall not create, delete, or rewrite ledger artifacts other than through the ledger's public typed operations.
- **FR-057:** A pass that fails at pass level shall leave every previously acknowledged durable outcome intact and observable after restart.
- **FR-058:** Watcher errors and diagnostics shall use stable categories and shall never contain claim tokens, raw JSON, external bodies, or unredacted operating-system messages containing caller data.
- **FR-059:** Every `readRecord`, `acquireClaim`, `renewClaim`, `checkpoint`, and `releaseClaim` response, and discovery-mode `initializeRecord` response, shall undergo method-specific runtime validation, not an `ok: true` cast. Validate the complete latest public record projection, canonical work-item identity, known outcome discriminator, accepted timestamp/revision/replay metadata, and owner/token/fencing/request consistency where applicable. Validate every known ledger error variant and its required fields; unknown categories, malformed variants, throws, and rejections become only `ledger-fault`, never arbitrary `error.kind` text. A non-cleanup fault produces `failed` / `ledger-unavailable`; cleanup follows FR-060. A replay's original accepted revision may be lower than its valid latest record revision. A malformed acquire establishes no authority and cannot fabricate a release; malformed checkpoint or initialization acknowledgements suppress all unacknowledged effects even if storage mutated durably, potentially losing their signals. Initialization validation binds the original authorized request, exact initial phase/status, initialized outcome and accepted revision/timestamp without inventing claim authority; valid initialization replay is not permission to return discovery-only intake again.
- **FR-060:** Every candidate shall report cleanup status/reason/category/revision independently of its primary outcome. Failed or indeterminate cleanup promotes an otherwise nonfailed candidate to `failed` / `ledger-unavailable` for `ledger-fault`, otherwise `failed` / `claim-cleanup-unconfirmed`. Preserve an existing primary failed reason. Preserve acknowledged checkpoint/initialization revision, cursor changes when present, and returned intake signal; no rollback or retraction. Initialization alone follows FR-069's no-acquisition cleanup rule.

### Discovery Scope, Matching and Admission (Approved; Unimplemented/Unreviewed)

- **FR-061 — Mode and host boundary (D3-A):** Discovery shall be an explicit alternative request mode using an injected read-only, transport-neutral page seam. The host declares scope/partition identity, stability for the invocation, policy version and finite page/item/collection/UTF-8 bounds. Sandcastle shall not formulate live ADO queries, resolve teams from a service, authorize implementation or choose a lifecycle state.
- **FR-062 — Restart and durable state (D3-A):** Every invocation starts at the beginning of its declared host-stable scope/partition. A page continuation is opaque, invocation-local and usable only for that traversal; it is never persisted, exposed as a durable resume token, or stored in comment cursors. Progress across invocations consists only of existing durable per-item initialization/receipts and comment checkpoints. No schema/ADR-0025 storage change, sidecar, outbox or extra ledger field is permitted. The host must choose a stable scope/partition whose full traversal fits declared bounds; perpetual prefix rescans are not completion.
- **FR-063 — Bounded complete traversal (D3-A):** Validate each page before retaining or acting on its contents. Explicit terminal evidence, not an empty item array, ends traversal. Empty continued pages consume budget and continue. Page budget `P` permits at most `P` page-seam invocations per pass, including failed or empty calls; check the remaining budget and fresh cancellation before each call. Item overflow, duplicate canonical items, malformed/repeated continuations or unstable scope evidence fail closed with an explicit incomplete reason; do not truncate and report success. Preserve earlier validated per-item outcomes, report page calls/items processed and distinguish traversal completeness from candidate success. A complete traversal can contain skipped/failed dispositions. Retries never reset invocation budgets.
- **FR-064 — Collection and byte bounds (D2/D3-A):** Discovery requests declare positive safe-integer limits for pages, total discovered items (at most 1,000), entries per page, each filter/fact collection, individual opaque values, and aggregate UTF-8 bytes for policy input and each page including required matching facts and continuation. Validate inclusive bounds before copying/iterating collections and incrementally before retaining values; no oversized full projection, truncation or silent deduplication. Both exactly `N` entries / `B` UTF-8 bytes and `N+1` / `B+1` are conformance boundaries. Multibyte values are measured as UTF-8, not character count. Existing canonical ledger ID and comment-window limits (FR-035b) remain unchanged and cannot be raised by discovery configuration. Invalid configuration has zero injected effects; malformed or over-bound page/fact data permits no admission or observation for that page. A structurally valid missing-fact indication instead pauses only its candidate under FR-065/066.
- **FR-065 — Matching semantics (D2):** AND all configured dimensions; within an allowed-state set or allowed-team set, OR its members. `tagsAll`, `tagsAny` and `tagsNone`, when supplied, are nonempty: respectively require all, at least one, or none of their values; combine these configured predicates with AND. State and team sets likewise must be nonempty when configured. Compare host-normalized opaque values exactly and case-sensitively, without implicit substring matching, locale folding, trimming or inferred synonyms. Area/iteration filters explicitly select exact path or segment-aware subtree; subtree includes the root and descendant segments, never a textual prefix sibling. The host supplies normalized path segments/boundaries; the watcher does not infer separators. Team membership is host-resolved and never inferred from `assignedTo`. Omitted dimensions impose no restriction. Missing required facts fail closed with a stable reason (a known empty collection is not a missing fact); unsupported operators are configuration errors, not ignored filters.
- **FR-066 — Eligibility pause and reentry (D2-A):** Evaluate required matching facts before privacy projection and before comment/PR observation, admission or claim acquisition. An excluded or fact-incomplete candidate is paused/skipped with policy-versioned minimized evidence, zero comment/PR calls and no record/cursor mutation. A paused record retains its last durable anchor; no pause marker is persisted. On a later matching invocation, resume from that anchor, never from a new baseline or inferred latest event. Retention loss must be explicit `observation-anchor-missing`, not an empty-success fallback or automatic reset. The host seam must distinguish known retention loss from an ordinary no-new-events empty window. FR-042 phase/status suppression is applied only after observation eligibility and still advances cursors.
- **FR-067 — Authorized first admission (D1-B):** An eligible item with a validated missing-record result may call existing `initializeRecord` only when the host explicitly authorizes admission for that canonical item and supplies its exact initial phase and status. No authorization or missing required state means zero initialization and zero discovery-only intake with a stable disposition. Matching policy, ADO state, team membership or a ledger claim alone is not admission or execution authority. Initialize only the host-authorized workflow values and identity/idempotency fields; leave comment/PR cursors unset and invent no execution or claim metadata. A no-comment item is equally admissible. Existing records are never reinitialized or overwritten; all their mutations retain claimed, fenced, revision/state-checked semantics.
- **FR-068 — At-most-once admission reporting (D1-B):** Return a discovery-only signal only for a method-specifically validated fresh (`replayed: false`) durable initialization acceptance whose phase/status satisfy intake rules. Only one concurrent initialization can be fresh. Existing-record outcomes, initialization replays, retries after ambiguous acknowledgement and restart reads must not reconstruct or reemit that signal. Validated replay may establish the original durable outcome for reconciliation, not delivery; malformed/ambiguous acknowledgements establish neither delivered intake nor claim authority. Initialization that committed without a usable acknowledgement, or a crash before consumption, may lose intake permanently; this is deliberate at-most-once, not exactly-once. Never change an operation ID to evade a conflict or synthesize a checkpoint for delivery tracking.
- **FR-069 — Minimized evidence and cancellation (D1-B/D2/D3-A):** Results shall distinguish admission accepted, already recorded/replayed, unauthorized, matching-paused/facts-missing and incomplete traversal using stable typed reasons. Policy evidence identifies the exact host policy version and predicate/reason categories, never raw state/tag/path/team values, admission capabilities, page tokens, bodies or credentials. Sensitive matching happens in bounded memory before projection; neither facts nor policy contents enter durable artifacts, diagnostics or errors. Apply FR-051–053 and FR-059–060 to discovery pages and initialization too: gate new calls/mutations on fresh cancellation, quarantine late seam results, await in-flight ledger operations, preserve validated acknowledgements, and never manufacture durability or cleanup success. Initialization without any acquisition has cleanup `not-required` / `no-claim-acquired`, even if initialization acceptance is uncertain; that cleanup status proves no claim was acquired, not that initialization failed or succeeded.

### Mandatory Cleanup Contract

Every candidate outcome contains `cleanup: { status, reason, ledgerErrorKind, acceptedRevision }`. Status is `released | failed | indeterminate | not-required`; reason is `release-acknowledged | release-rejected | release-indeterminate | authority-unvalidated | no-claim-acquired`. `ledgerErrorKind` is a validated `DevSquadAdoLedgerError["kind"]`, `ledger-fault`, or null; `acceptedRevision` is a positive safe integer or null.

| Evidence                                                   | Status / reason                           | Category / accepted revision                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Validated release acknowledgement (including exact replay) | `released` / `release-acknowledged`       | null / original accepted release revision                           |
| Known release rejection, including `storage` / `unchanged` | `failed` / `release-rejected`             | validated category / null                                           |
| Release `storage` / `indeterminate` or `contention`        | `indeterminate` / `release-indeterminate` | validated category / null                                           |
| Release throws, rejects, or is malformed                   | `indeterminate` / `release-indeterminate` | `ledger-fault` / null                                               |
| Faulting or ambiguous acquire with no validated authority  | `indeterminate` / `authority-unvalidated` | `ledger-fault` or validated ambiguous category / null; zero release |
| No acquired claim and no acquisition uncertainty           | `not-required` / `no-claim-acquired`      | null / null; zero release                                           |

A replay acknowledges the original release, not absence of a later owner. Validate the original operation metadata and the complete latest record without requiring that latest record to have no active claim. For comment intake, `sourceRevision` is pre-acquire, outcome `revision` records the acknowledged checkpoint revision when present, and cleanup `acceptedRevision` is the original release revision; never replace one with another. Discovery-only intake cites accepted initialization revision instead, has no claim, and uses the no-acquisition cleanup row. Uncertain initialization is reported separately and must not be mislabeled as uncertain claim acquisition or acknowledged durability.

A token-free proposal returned to the DevSquad host for lifecycle interpretation, never execution authorization. The approved extension requires a discriminated distinction between the existing comment-intake shape and discovery-only intake; discovery fields must not be forced into the historical comment contract.

| Work item ID    | Canonical identifier                                |
| Source revision | Durable revision the signal was derived from        |
| Changed kinds   | `work-item-comment`, `pull-request-thread`, or both |
| Phase/status    | Exact caller-defined values read from the record    |
| Claim metadata  | Token-free claim owner, fencing value, and expiry   |

**Discovery-only intake (approved; unimplemented/unreviewed):**

| Attribute         | Description                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Kind              | Distinct discovery-only discriminator, not a comment observation kind                      |
| Work item ID      | Canonical identifier                                                                       |
| Accepted revision | Validated fresh initialization acceptance revision, not pre-acquire or checkpoint revision |
| Phase/status      | Exact initial caller-supplied workflow values admitted by intake rules                     |
| Policy evidence   | Host policy version and minimized match/admission reason categories                        |

Discovery-only signals contain no changed-comment kinds, cursors, source claim or claim metadata. Both signal types are returned at most once per candidate per pass; first initialization ends that item's step and later passes observe genuine comments normally. Neither type is a durable dispatch queue.

### Observation Snapshot

The injected, in-memory view of one candidate's external activity for one poll: ordered opaque work-item comment identifiers and ordered opaque pull-request thread/comment cursors. Snapshots are never persisted. Discovery matching facts are separate bounded in-memory inputs, evaluated before privacy projection and never converted to comment identifiers or persisted in snapshots.

### Discovery Page and Traversal Result

A page belongs to a host-declared stable scope/partition and supplies bounded canonical item identities, required host-normalized matching facts, and an explicit terminal marker or opaque invocation-local continuation. Raw facts and continuation never enter public results. A traversal result reports counts and complete/incomplete with a stable reason, retaining per-item dispositions. It is not a restart cursor and makes no claim about unvisited items in an incomplete scope.

### Eligibility Decision

The pure result of evaluating one candidate: eligible or ineligible with a stable reason. Supplied-candidate observation retains its new-event decision. Discovery additionally separates policy eligibility, authorized first admission and comment newness; policy evidence is tied to the exact version. Exclusion pauses observation, whereas phase/status suppression gates intake after observation.

## Invariants

- **INV-001 — Injected boundary:** Every external observation passes through the injected seam; the watcher performs no network, MCP, CLI, git, sandbox, or agent operation.
- **INV-002 — Read-only externally:** A watch pass never mutates external tracker state.
- **INV-003 — Deterministic decisions:** Identical inputs, clock readings, and durable state always yield identical decisions, ordering, and reason codes.
- **INV-004 — Single active owner:** Mutations of existing records require current unexpired claimed/fenced authority. The sole creation exception is explicitly authorized discovery initialization through existing claim-free `initializeRecord`; creation neither grants authority nor permits overwriting an existing record.
- **INV-005 — Fenced writes:** A superseded fencing value can never advance a cursor or write a checkpoint.
- **INV-006 — Non-duplicating intake:** Each externally observed comment event yields at most one intake signal across passes because cursor advancement precedes delivery reporting. A first-seen admission independently yields at most one discovery-only signal because only validated fresh initialization can report it; replay/restart/existing-record discovery cannot reemit. Both are at-most-once, not lossless: durable-but-unacknowledged acceptance or a crash may permanently lose a signal.
- **INV-007 — Durable-before-report:** Comment intake requires validated durable checkpoint acceptance. Discovery-only intake instead requires validated fresh durable initialization acceptance, with no invented checkpoint, comment event or claim. No `acted` outcome rests solely on a match, attempted write, replayed admission or ambiguous acknowledgement.
- **INV-008 — Idempotent steps:** Replaying a watcher step's operation identifier cannot create a duplicate revision, checkpoint, or cursor advance; and no operation identifier is ever reused for a mutation the ledger would have to reject as an idempotency conflict.
- **INV-009 — Lifecycle separation:** The watcher records and reports phase and status but never determines their legality, ordering, or next value.
- **INV-010 — Data minimization:** No external body, credential, token, prompt, or session content enters durable state, results, logs, or errors.
- **INV-011 — Bounded scheduling:** Poll/count/window and discovery page/item/collection/UTF-8 bounds limit watcher work; incomplete traversal is explicit and invocation-local continuations are not durable progress. Liveness assumes settling ledger operations and delays, and observation timeout assumes a valid timer. Noncooperating seams cannot be forcibly terminated; a page-call initiation budget is not an unconditional runtime or downstream physical-request bound.
- **INV-012 — Claim hygiene:** Exactly one release invocation follows previously validated authority on candidate exit; no validated authority means zero release. Cleanup evidence is mandatory and truthful; inclusive expiry is the backstop and another owner's claim is never force-released.
- **INV-013 — Fail-closed recovery:** Corrupt, unsupported, or capacity-blocked records are reported and isolated, never repaired or reset by the watcher.
- **INV-014 — Candidate isolation:** One candidate's failure never corrupts, blocks, or alters another candidate's durable state.
- **INV-015 — No cursor regression:** A checkpoint never moves an observation cursor to a position derived from anchors that are no longer durable; an observation invalidated between its read and its claim is abandoned, not written.
- **INV-016 — Typed injected boundaries:** No injected dependency — seam, ledger, clock, or delay — can escape the pass as an untyped throw, and none of their raw values reach results, errors, or diagnostics.
- **INV-017 — Host-owned admission and matching:** Matching uses only explicit host policy and required facts; unknown facts fail closed. Neither policy match nor local initialization grants authority to implement, schedule agents or choose lifecycle state.
- **INV-018 — Pause is not suppression:** Discovery exclusion leaves durable cursors unchanged and performs no comment observation; reentry uses the original durable anchor or explicit retention error. FR-042 suppression remains cursor-advancing for otherwise observation-eligible records.
- **INV-019 — No discovery persistence:** Every discovery invocation starts at its host scope/partition beginning. Only existing ledger per-item records/receipts/checkpoints survive; no continuation, matching facts, sidecar or new storage schema is introduced.

## Success Criteria

- **SC-001:** Across 1,000 repeated passes with identical inputs and durable state, 100% produce byte-identical decision sets, reason codes, and counts.
- **SC-002:** Across at least 1,000 overlapping two-owner comment-observation trials on one eligible existing work item with valid dependencies, exactly one owner acts in every trial. Discovery admission races instead meet SC-011 and do not manufacture local claims.
- **SC-003:** In stale-owner testing, 0% of superseded-fencing mutations advance a cursor or change a revision.
- **SC-004:** For any observed comment event or first-seen admission, **at most one** corresponding typed intake signal is delivered across passes; duplicate delivery rate is 0%. Delivery is not lossless: a checkpoint or initialization that becomes durable without usable acknowledgement can lose its signal permanently, so completeness must be reconciled from the durable record, not from the signal stream.
- **SC-005:** All tested passes enforce scheduling/window bounds and cooperative cancellation under settling ledger/delay dependencies. No unconditional runtime/physical-request guarantee is inferred from these tests.
- **SC-006:** Every candidate reports cleanup; exactly one release is attempted for validated authority and none for unvalidated authority. Cleanup counts partition outcomes; no foreign force-release.
- **SC-007:** Automated scanning of durable artifacts, results, errors, and diagnostics finds zero external bodies, credentials, or claim tokens.
- **SC-008:** In candidate-isolation testing, a single failing candidate never changes another candidate's durable revision, in 100% of trials.
- **SC-009:** An operator can determine from one pass result why every candidate was acted on, skipped, suppressed, or failed, without reading agent-session contents or external bodies.
- **SC-010:** The full watcher test suite completes without live ADO, GitHub, MCP, network, Azure CLI, Azure DevOps CLI, sandbox, agent-provider, or git dependencies, and without wall-clock waiting.
- **SC-011:** In the item `999` no-comment admission matrix, every authorized fresh valid acceptance yields one discovery-only intake when intake rules admit it, and the combined concurrency/retry/restart count never exceeds one; every unauthorized variant performs zero initializations. No fake cursor, checkpoint or claim appears. Covers CC-031.
- **SC-012:** In 100% of exclusion/reentry cases, cursor `480` remains untouched while `481` arrives; reentry resumes from `480` or reports explicit retention loss, while the phase/status-suppression control still advances. Covers CC-032/033.
- **SC-013:** All bounded multi-page `137`/`138` fixtures report complete dispositions and restart from the beginning with zero duplicate acknowledged comment intake. Every page-budget-`2` case initiates at most two calls and reports incomplete when continued; no empty continued page is treated as terminal. Covers CC-034/035.
- **SC-014:** Every exact configured collection/UTF-8 bound is accepted for otherwise valid inputs, every `+1` is rejected before item effects/oversized retention, and cancellation/privacy matrices find zero late seam-triggered mutations, leakage, external writes or fabricated durable/cleanup acknowledgements. Covers CC-036/037.

## Assumptions

- **Candidate supply:** The DevSquad host chooses either the supplied set or a stable bounded discovery scope/partition and policy. Discovery executes only injected read-oriented page traversal; live queries and selection authority remain host-owned. **Owner:** DevSquad host integration.
- **Record initialization:** Supplied mode requires host-preinitialized records. Discovery may initialize first-seen items only on explicit host authorization and exact initial phase/status through unchanged `initializeRecord`; no implicit lifecycle defaults or implementation authority. **Owner:** DevSquad host integration.
- **Matching facts:** The host provides exact normalized values, explicit normalized path segments and resolved team memberships. Required missing facts fail closed; matching precedes privacy projection and evidence references the exact policy version without raw facts. **Owner:** Host seam implementer.
- **Discovery stability and bounds:** The host supplies a stable scope/partition traversable within declared finite budgets. Each invocation restarts at the beginning; inadequate bounds or unstable traversal produce explicit incompletion, not durable pagination or eventual-progress guarantees. **Owner:** DevSquad host integration.
- **Observation ordering:** The injected seam returns observations in the tracker's authoritative order, and the watcher trusts that order rather than parsing identifiers. **Owner:** Host seam implementer.
- **Anchor retention:** The seam window is anchor-inclusive: whenever a `since` anchor is supplied and the window is non-empty, the anchor appears in it. The tracker keeps anchored entries retrievable while they are durable cursors, including during eligibility pauses; discovery reentry requires explicit retention-loss evidence if that obligation cannot be met, even when the response would otherwise be empty. Anchors are compared by equality only. The watcher fails closed with `observation-anchor-missing`, never resets/rebaselines automatically, and re-anchoring is a deliberate host action. Ordinary empty no-new-event behavior in supplied mode remains unchanged. **Owner:** Host seam implementer.
- **Pass identity:** The host supplies a stable pass identity; the watcher never generates one. Checkpoint identifiers are reproduced by a retry, while claim-lifecycle identifiers are freshly scoped per acquisition, so the same `passId` is always safe to retry. **Owner:** DevSquad host integration.
- **Time basis:** Timestamps are UTC and supplied by the injected clock; tests control time deterministically. **Owner:** Sandcastle planning.
- **Workload scale:** A single pass examines no more than 1,000 candidates, within ledger schema-v1 capacity bounds. **Owner:** Product maintainer.
- **Delivery semantics:** Intake signals are consumed in-process by the caller of the pass; the watcher provides no queue, retry, or transport. **Owner:** DevSquad host integration.
- **Liveness:** Ledger methods and delays settle; observation timers behave as valid delays. Abort requests cooperation, not forcible termination, and late noncooperating operations may remain physically in flight. **Owner:** Host dependency implementer.
- **Reconciliation:** At-most-once batched intake can lose signals after durable-unacknowledged writes or host crashes; reconcile existing per-item initialization receipts/records and comment cursors against intake processing without adding an outbox or reconstructing discovery delivery on replay. **Owner:** DevSquad host integration.
- **Single-machine coordination:** Concurrent watchers share the same repository filesystem, per ADR-0025. **Owner:** Deployment owner.

## Dependencies and ADR Alignment

- **ADR-0025 — DevSquad/ADO workflow ledger:** The watcher is a pure consumer of the ledger's public typed operations, claim/lease/fencing/idempotency rules, and fail-closed recovery behavior. It introduces no new persistence format and no schema change.
- **ADR-0026 — Watcher baseline and amendment checkpoint:** The Proposed ADR documents the technically reviewed supplied-candidate behavior. This approved spec extends its supplied-only/no-initialization seam and responsibility wording for discovery, but does not retroactively amend or accept that ADR. Reconciliation of ADR-0026 and the plan/tasks belongs to the subsequent explicitly requested plan transition before implementation; no ADR or code change is part of this checkpoint. ADR-0025's existing claim-free initialization and storage remain unchanged.
- **ADR-0024 — DevSquad Sandcastle execution adapter boundary:** DevSquad retains work-item lifecycle, phase legality, scheduling, and pull-request authority; the watcher only proposes intake.
- **ADR-0021 — ADO feedback-loop control-plane boundary:** All external observation stays behind an injected, structurally validated seam with no credentials, transport, or network coupling in Sandcastle core.
- **ADR-0022 — ADO agent-team runner boundary:** The watcher does not schedule or execute agents; it may inform a host runner.
- **ADR-0012 — Agent-provider-owned session storage:** Only session identifiers already present in the ledger are surfaced; the watcher never reads session contents.
- **ADR-0007 — Worktree locking:** Precedent for host-side ownership; watcher exclusivity is provided by ledger claims, not worktree locks.
